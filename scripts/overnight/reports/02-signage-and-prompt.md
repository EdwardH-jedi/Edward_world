**Readings taken.**
- *"The object's top edge"* is the art under the chip, not the box. The boxes start in sky (the Arcade has 6 empty rows; SportsGang's middle is 21 rows below its box top), so a box-anchored sign still floats. The sign's height is measured for the centred chip, so it never jumps when the chip clamps sideways.
- *House — the one sign that moved **up**:* the chimney (row 0) is under the chip, so the sign hangs above it with an ~8-unit post down to the ridge, which reads as a rooftop sign. At 1280×800 it is about **9 units higher than before** (y≈7 vs y≈70; the old clamp sat it over the chimney stack). Its natural top equals the crop line, and the 1u floor pushes it down one, so its shadow touches the chimney and there is no clear unit. **Alternative for the reviewer:** anchor the House to its ridge (row 7) and let the chip cover the chimney stack, as the old clamp effectively did. That costs the "sign covers no art" check for that one object in `tests/signage.test.ts`.
- *"One unit gap"* is counted below the offset shadow: chip, shadow, 1 clear unit, art (`k+5`). If the gap sat directly under the border, the ink shadow would fill it and a char stem would vanish.
- *Stem and tail* are empty `aria-hidden` spans, not `::after`, so the grid spec can measure them; label text and accessible names are unchanged.
- *"Bubble wins, label unchanged":* the bubble draws over every sign (it's a child of the player, z 3). Where it would cover any sign it lifts whole units until 1u of air shows. Signs never move or hide. At the stations, which are Edward's height, it always lifts.
- The bubble keeps its text visible to assistive tech (not hidden), as the old prompt did. The status bar is untouched.
